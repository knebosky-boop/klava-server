Privacy policy

klava-server accesses Google Drive only on behalf of its single owner and only to copy the owner's own files between the owner's Google Drive and the owner's own server.

  • No data is sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties.
  • No data is used for advertising, analytics or model training.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account settings (Security → Third-party apps).

Last updated: 27 September 2026.
